Papyris helps small businesses that sell on WhatsApp manage customers, track
sales, take mobile money payments and follow up on deliveries, all from one
simple app that works on your phone.

### What it does

- **Customer profiles:** contact details, notes, tags, orders, payments and
  reminders on one page per customer.
- **Sales pipeline:** a drag-and-drop board that moves every deal from new lead
  to completed.
- **One-tap WhatsApp:** message any customer without copying numbers around.
- **Orders, invoices and receipts:** payment links, PDFs and sharing on WhatsApp.
- **Mobile money:** payment statuses tracked from sent to paid.
- **Deliveries and follow-ups:** delivery tracking and reminders so no lead goes
  cold.
- **Storefront:** a shareable shop page where customers can browse and order.
- **Calendar, support tickets and reports:** with Google Calendar sync.

Works in any browser, with apps for iOS and Android. Every account starts with a
14-day free trial.
